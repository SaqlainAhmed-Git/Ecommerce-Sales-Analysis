# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing E-Commerce sales data to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand profitability, and uncover insights across different product categories, subcategories, and geographical regions.

An interactive dashboard was developed to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate category-wise sales and profitability.
- Identify the top-performing product subcategories.
- Analyze sales distribution across different US states.
- Provide interactive filters for business users.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. **Sales and Profit Analysis** – Monthly comparison of sales and profit.
2. **Category-wise Profit Analysis** – Profit contribution across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
3. **Category-wise Sales Share** – Percentage contribution of each product category to total sales.
4. **Sales by State** – Geographical analysis of sales performance across the United States.
5. **Top 5 Subcategories by Sales** – Highlights the highest revenue-generating subcategories.
6. **Interactive Filters** – Year, Region, and Customer Segment filters for dynamic analysis.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps answer important business questions:

- Which months generate the highest sales?
- Which product category contributes the most profit?
- Which subcategories generate the highest revenue?
- How are sales distributed across different states?
- How does business performance change across years and regions?
- What is the overall profitability of the business?
